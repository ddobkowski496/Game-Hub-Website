How it works

Laps 5-lap Grand Prix, rolling start from the grid
Damage Wall & car contact deforms bodywork, cuts aero & power
Tyres Soft / Medium / Hard compounds wear at different rates — grip fades as they wear
Fuel A heavy load slows you down; low fuel leans the engine out
DRS & tow Slipstream the car ahead; press F inside a DRS zone within 1 second to open the flap
Racing line Green = flat out, yellow = lift, red = braking zone
Repairs Stop in the pit lane: crew repairs damage, fits fresh tyres and refuels
Gears 8-speed gearbox, torque curve, redline at 13,500 rpm
